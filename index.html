Account Deletion Architecture & Workflow

Complete technical specification of endpoint payloads, cascade data purge, rate-limiting, and cooling-off period rules.

1. Executive Summary & Google Play Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy Requirements regarding User Data & In-App Account Deletion and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, OFF Mela provides an automated, programmatic, and permanent account deletion service.

  • Self-Service Erasure: Customers and Merchants can delete their accounts directly inside the app without submitting a manual support ticket.
  • Administrative Deletion: Authorized Admins and Super Admins can delete fraudulent, abusive, or offline-requested accounts from the Admin Portal.
  • Privileged Backend Execution: Deletions are processed by an isolated microservice (`Offer Server`) utilizing Google Cloud Functions and a verified Firebase Admin SDK Service Account.
  • Permanent Cascade: Authentication tokens are revoked, auth records deleted, and all Firestore profile records, notifications, shops, and offers purged.

2. System Architecture

6. Cascade Data Cleanup Workflow

When the endpoint executes, the following sequential purge takes place:

  1. Token Verification: Decodes and validates the caller's Firebase ID token using admin.auth().verifyIdToken().
  2. Role & Authorization Check: If targetUid !== callerUid, verifies the caller's admin privileges via custom claims or admins collection.
  3. Merchant Cascade (If Merchant):
    • Locates shops where ownerUid == targetUid in shops.
    • Queries and batch-deletes all promotional offers in offers tied to the merchant's UID or shopId.
    • Deletes pending or approved boost requests in boost_requests.
    • Deletes the merchant's store document in shops.
  4. Subcollections Purge:
    • Deletes all documents in users/{targetUid}/notifications.
    • Deletes all documents in users/{targetUid}/saved_offers.
    • Deletes all documents in users/{targetUid}/saved_shops.
    • Deletes all documents in users/{targetUid}/redemptions.
  5. Primary Document Deletion: Deletes users/{targetUid} and removes entry from admins if applicable.
  6. Authentication Destruction: Permanently deletes user from Firebase Auth via admin.auth().deleteUser(targetUid), revoking all refresh tokens.
  7. Statutory Audit Log: Writes an anonymized record to account_deletion_logs capturing timestamp, initiating UID, reason, and deletion summary.

7. Deletion Frequency & 30-Day Cooling-Off Policy

How often are users permitted to delete their account without concern?
Any genuine customer or merchant may delete their account at any time without penalty, fee, or concern.

Anti-Abuse & Promotional Integrity Safeguards

  • The 30-Day Cooling-Off Window: When an account is deleted, a non-reversible cryptographic hash of the primary identifier (phone or email) is recorded in account_deletion_logs alongside a 30-day timestamp.
  • Returning User Classification: If an individual re-registers with the same phone or email within this 30-day window, the system automatically classifies them as a Returning Subscriber.
  • Promotional Perk Suppression: One-time introductory benefits (such as new user welcome discounts, inaugural coupon bonuses, or first-time merchant boost credits) are suppressed during the cooling-off window. This completely eliminates promotional coupon cycling without restricting legitimate users.
  • Rapid Cycling Threshold: A single identifier may perform a maximum of 3 deletion-and-recreation cycles within a 90-day period. Accounts exceeding this threshold are placed on a 60-day registration hold requiring administrative review.
  • Admin Exemption: Platform administrators are exempt from cooling-off limitations when deleting accounts for moderation, customer assistance, or fraud containment.
